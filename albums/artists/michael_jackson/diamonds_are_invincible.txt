Released on August 29, 2018, Diamonds are Invincible was created to celebrate what would have been Michael Jackson’s 60th birthday. Sony Music and the Jackson Estate brought in Oscar and Grammy winning producer Mark Ronson to weave eight of Michael’s biggest hits into a single five minute megamix. Ronson aimed to capture the energy of a wild, crowded dance floor by seamlessly blending classics like "Don't Stop 'Til You Get Enough," "Billie Jean," and "Smooth Criminal".
When news of the track broke, fans and music lovers had mixed reactions. Remaking or editing iconic tracks from a deceased legend always carries a risk. Some listeners feared it would feel like a cheap cash grab or a quick marketing stunt by the estate to profit off a milestone birthday. Others worried that slicing up such famous records would ruin the original magic that made those tracks special.
Once the track dropped, however, the response was surprisingly positive. Ronson respected the original vocal tracks, letting Michael’s raw voice lead the way while stitching the beats together with smooth club transitions. Instead of coming across as a lazy cash grab, it played like a fun, high energy tribute that got old and new fans dancing to the King of Pop all over again.
