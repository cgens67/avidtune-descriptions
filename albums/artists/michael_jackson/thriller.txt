There are very few pop albums, or works of art of any kind, that have made as much of an impact on the world as Michael Jackson's Thriller did in 1982.
Produced alongside Quincy Jones, the album hits a rare balance where every single track serves a purpose. It starts strong with the driving energy of "Wanna Be Startin' Somethin'" before shifting to the playful Paul McCartney duet "The Girl Is Mine" and the clean synth-pop rhythm of "Baby Be Mine."
The record really finds its edge on side two. Eddie Van Halen lends a iconic rock guitar solo to "Beat It," while "Billie Jean" delivers one of the most recognizable basslines ever recorded. The title track, "Thriller," brings a fun theatrical vibe, and "P.Y.T. (Pretty Young Thing)" keeps the dance floor moving with its sharp funk hooks.
The softer side of the record is just as strong. "Human Nature" stands out as a tender, reflective highlight, while the quiet groove of "The Lady in My Life" closes things out on a soulful note.
Decades after its release, Thriller still holds up as the best-selling album of all time, setting a standard for pop music that few have ever managed to match.
