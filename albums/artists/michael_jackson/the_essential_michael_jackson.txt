Released in 2005 by Epic and Legacy Recordings, The Essential Michael Jackson was created as a major career overview. The streaming version features 32 tracks spanning over two hours of music, tracing his journey from early hits with The Jackson 5 and The Jacksons straight through his solo dominance across iconic albums like Off the Wall, Thriller, Bad, Dangerous, and HIStory.
When the album was announced, public reaction was mixed. It hit shelves right as Michael’s high profile trial was coming to an end in mid 2005. Because of the timing, critics and casual listeners initially viewed the release as a cynical cash grab by Sony Music. Many felt the label was attempting to capitalize on the massive media attention and squeeze quick sales out of fans by repackaging hits everyone already owned.
However, once people focused on the tracklist itself, perceptions changed. The Essential stood out because it clearly connected his early group era classics, like "Enjoy Yourself" and "Blame It on the Boogie", with his defining solo work like "Billie Jean" and "Smooth Criminal". It ended up being one of his most successful greatest hits releases, earning multi platinum certifications globally and proving that the timeless power of his music could outshine the headlines.
