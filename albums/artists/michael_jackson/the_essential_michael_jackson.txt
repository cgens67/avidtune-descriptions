Released in 2005 by Epic and Legacy Recordings, The Essential Michael Jackson was put together as a massive two disc compilation spanning 38 tracks across his career. It covers everything from his childhood hits with The Jackson 5 and The Jacksons all the way through his defining solo albums like Off the Wall, Thriller, Bad, Dangerous, and HIStory.
When the compilation was announced, public reaction was mixed. The album was released right around the time Michael’s high profile trial was coming to an end. Because of the timing, plenty of critics and fans saw it as a transparent cash grab by Sony Music. People questioned whether the record label was just trying to capitalize on the media circus and squeeze extra sales out of casual listeners by repackaging songs everyone already owned.
However, once the focus turned back to the actual music, opinions shifted. Unlike previous best of sets, The Essential stood out because it seamlessly connected his early Motown and Epic group era hits, like "Enjoy Yourself" and "Blame It on the Boogie", directly with his solo pop dominance. It turned out to be one of the most complete overviews of his legacy ever put together, going multi platinum worldwide and proving that his musical impact could still outshine the headlines.
