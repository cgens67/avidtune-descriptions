Released on November 18, 2003, by Epic Records, Number Ones was assembled as a definitive collection of Michael Jackson’s chart topping singles from around the world. The 18 track album covers his legendary solo run under Sony, including fundamental hits like "Don't Stop 'Til You Get Enough," "Billie Jean," "Bad," and "Smooth Criminal," alongside the brand new single "One More Chance" written by R. Kelly.
At the time of its release, the project faced heavy scrutiny from music critics and the public. In late 2003, Michael was locked in a severe conflict with Sony Music leadership, and the compilation was widely seen as a corporate obligation to fulfill his multi album contractual commitment. On top of that, major news stories surrounding his personal life broke during the exact week the album hit store shelves. Because of this chaotic timing, many people dismissed Number Ones as a quick cash grab by the label to capitalize on sensational media headlines rather than celebrate his music.
Despite the controversy and lack of traditional promotional appearances, the music spoke for itself. Fans rallied around the album, turning it into a massive commercial success that went multi platinum across the globe. The compilation remained a fan favorite for decades, eventually breaking sales records in 2009 as one of the best selling catalog albums in music history.
